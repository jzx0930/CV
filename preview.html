Equipment control software engineer. My work sits between the machine and the operator: motion controllers, laser sources, industrial cameras and pneumatics on one side, and a C# / .NET WinForms interface a technician can actually use on the other. Two complementary tracks — equipment control software (multi-axis motion control, fieldbus integration, fault tolerance and threading design) and machine vision (acquisition, edge detection, segmentation and object recognition with Emgu CV / OpenCV). A mechanical engineering background followed by bio-industrial mechatronics, plus a year of hands-on PLC and cleanroom work in semiconductor automation, lets me follow a problem from the mechanism and the wiring up to the user interface. I trust logs and measurements over assumptions.
